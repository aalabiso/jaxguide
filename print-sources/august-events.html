☀️ August Weather

August is Jacksonville's hottest month — expect highs in the low 90s, high humidity, and afternoon thunderstorms (the "4 o'clock shower" is a daily ritual). Mornings and evenings are your best bet for outdoor activities. Hit the beach early, seek A/C midday, then enjoy golden-hour dining al fresco. Stay hydrated!

🌊 August Beach Tip

Sea turtle nesting season is in full swing — watch for marked nests and hatchling tracks, but keep your distance. The Atlantic is bathwater-warm (82°F+), perfect for swimming and paddleboarding. Watch for afternoon lightning. Jaguars Training Camp runs through Aug 26 at Miller Electric Center — free public practices most mornings at jaguars.com.
