🐬 About Jacksonville University: Founded in 1934, JU sits on a beautiful 260-acre campus along the St. Johns River. The Dolphins compete in NCAA Division I as part of the ASUN Conference. Famous alumni include NFL great Fred Taylor. The campus is in the Arlington neighborhood, about 10 minutes east of downtown Jacksonville.

🏖️

Beach Day: Jacksonville Beach is about 25 minutes from JU via Beach Blvd (US-90). The Atlantic is 82°F+ in August — perfect for swimming. Grab dinner at a beachside restaurant afterward. · Getting Around: JU is car-friendly, but Uber/Lyft are readily available throughout Jacksonville.
